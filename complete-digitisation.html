Frequently asked questions

What's included in the Complete Digitisation package?

Everything to get your business fully online and keep it there: a professional website, your own domain and business email, Google Business Profile and local SEO, logo and branding, Facebook and Instagram setup, online booking and automations, plus hosting, maintenance and support. How much does it cost?

The complete setup is a one-off £2,900, then £199 a month for hosting, maintenance, support and light ongoing SEO and social. We confirm everything in a clear, fixed quote — no hidden fees.

Most businesses are fully live within two to four weeks from kickoff, once we have your details and content. We handle the whole technical side from start to finish.

No. You tell us about your business; we set up the website, domain, email, Google, socials, booking and automations for you, and explain everything in plain English.

Yes. The monthly plan keeps your site hosted, updated, backed up and supported. You can change or cancel it with a month's notice, and your website and accounts always remain yours.
