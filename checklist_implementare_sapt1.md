## CONTENT BATCHING (DAYS 2-4)

### Gather Assets (Day 2)
- [ ] Find 5-10 best portfolio pieces (client sites with permission to share)
- [ ] Collect client testimonials (ask 3-5 clients for 30-60 sec video testimonial)

- [ ] Take photos/videos for BTS content:

## ENGAGEMENT HABITS (DAILY)

### Daily 15-Min Routine
- [ ] Check comments on posts (RESPOND PERSONALLY)
  - Every comment = opportunity to engage
  - Don't reply with auto-responses
  
- [ ] Engage with 5-10 posts from target audience
  - Follow relevant local business pages
  - Like + meaningful comment on 3-5 posts
  - (Algorithm loves active accounts)

- [ ] Monitor ad performance
  - Check click-through rate (aim for 2-5%)
  - Check cost per lead (aim for €5-15)
  - Pause underperforming variations

## COMMON PITFALLS TO AVOID

❌ Don't post once and disappear
✓ Commit to consistency (4 weeks minimum before judging)

❌ Don't expect leads overnight from ads
✓ First month is learning; scale what works in month 2

❌ Don't ignore comments
✓ Every comment is a micro-lead-gen opportunity

❌ Don't be salesy in educational content
✓ Give value first, sell soft (in CTA only)

❌ Don't copy competitors' content
✓ Use competitor strategies as inspiration, make it yours
