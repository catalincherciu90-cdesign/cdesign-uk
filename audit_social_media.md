# AUDIT SOCIAL MEDIA C-DESIGN
## Strategie Social Media & Plan Editorial

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

C-Design (c-design.ro) este o agenție web design din Ilfov care servește afaceri mici din România cu pachete de la 300€. Pentru a maximiza creșterea și a atrage clienți noi, avem o oportunitate semnificativă pe social media — în nișa web design B2B pentru PME, puțini jucători au o prezență social media puternică. Poți deveni lider de opinie local și național.

**Obiectiv principal:** Transformă social media într-un canal de generare de lead-uri și brand awareness. Target: antreprenori, freelanceri, mici proprietari de afaceri care nu au site sau au site vechi.

---

## 1. AUDIT PREZENȚĂ ACTUALĂ & PLATFORME PRIORITARE

### Platforma Prioritară 1: FACEBOOK (Priority 1)
**De ce:** 70% din antreprenorii mici sunt pe Facebook. Ads-urile sunt cel mai cost-efective pentru local targeting (Ilfov, București și toată România). Comunități de antreprenori și grupuri de afaceri sunt extrem active pe FB.

**Recomandare:** 
- Pagina business C-Design (dacă nu există, crează urgent)
- Postări: 3-4/săptămână
- Ads budget: 300-500€/lună (testat pe lead-uri calitate)
- Grup Facebook privat: "Antreprenori cu site-uri care vând" (community + lead magnet)

### Platforma Prioritară 2: INSTAGRAM (Priority 2)
**De ce:** Visual-centered, perfect pentru portofoliu website showcases. Story-uri + Reels cu case studies, before/after redesign, client testimonials.

**Recomandare:**
- Postări: 2-3/săptămână (Reels 1x/săptămână)
- Stories: 4-5x/săptămână (behind-the-scenes, quick tips, polls)
- Focus: Portfolio showcase + educational content despre web design

### Platforma Prioritară 3: LINKEDIN (Priority 3)
**De ce:** B2B credibility. Articole despre digital transformation, web design trends. Personal branding founder → company authority.

**Recomandare:**
- Postări: 1-2/săptămână
- Newsletter (LinkedIn Newsletter): 2x/lună
- Focus: Case studies, industry insights, thought leadership

### Platforma Opțională: TIKTOK (Priority 4 — starter)
**De ce:** Algoritm viral. Videoclipuri scurte despre web design mistakes, design trends, quick fixes pentru site-uri vechi.

**Recomandare:**
- Testare inițial: 1 video/săptămână
- Format: Short-form videos (15-30 sec) cu hook puternic

## 2. PILONI DE CONȚINUT (20 Idei Lunare Grupate)

### Pilonul 1: CASE STUDIES & PORTOFOLIU (5 postări/lună)

1. **Before/After Redesign Visual Showcase** (Instagram Reel + Carousel)

2. **Client Success Story — "Primul client a venit în luna 1"** (LinkedIn Long-form)
   - Interviu text/video cu client real (cu permisiune)
   - Rezultate concrete: X vizitatori/lună, Y contacte, Z vânzări

3. **Portfolio Flash Reel — 5 Site-uri în 30 Secunde** (Instagram/Facebook Reel)
   - Montaj rapid de site-uri lansat de C-Design
   - Muzică motivantă, text suprapus

4. **Client Testimonial Video (15-30 sec)** (Instagram Story, Reel, TikTok)
   - Client videoclip: "Cum a changed website my business?"
   - Subtitle text în română + engleză (viral potențial)

5. **Detailed Case Study Post** (Facebook + LinkedIn + Blog)
   - Lungă, SEO-optimizată: Client, Challenge, Solution, Results, Metrics

## 4. HASHTAG STRATEGY

### Facebook
- Minimă hashtag usage (1-2 generici): #WebDesignRomania, #DigitalMarketing
- Focus: Descriptive text, CTA, engaging question

### Instagram (Critical — Hashtags = Discovery)
**High-Volume Hashtags (100K-500K):**
- #webdesign #webdesigner #webdesignaward #websitedesign #designlogo

**Mid-Volume (10K-100K):**
- #webdesignromania #webdesignagency #sitedesign #freelancedesigner #designbusiness

**Niche/Local (1K-10K):**
- #webdesignilfov #webdesignbucuresti #webdesignromania #afacerimic #startupdesign

**Branded (Your own):**
- #CDesignWork #CDesignStudio #21DaysToLive (your process)

*Strategy:* 25-30 hashtags per post (mix of all 4 categories), place in first comment for cleaner appearance

### LinkedIn
- Minimal hashtag usage (3-5): #WebDesign #DigitalTransformation #SmallBusiness #Romania
- Instead: Use keywords in text, @mention relevant companies/people

### TikTok
- 5-8 trending + niche: #webdesign #designtips #entrepreneurs #startups #designmistakes

## 6. 5 IDEI DE CONȚINUT VIRAL (WEB DESIGN NICHE)

### Idea 1: "The Website Test That Shocked Me"
**Format:** 30-sec Reel / TikTok
**Hook:** "I tested 100 small business websites. Here's what broke the most."
**Content:** Show 3 common website mistakes side-by-side comparison with fix
**Why Viral:** Shock value + practical value + relatable (everyone has seen these mistakes)
**Platforms:** Instagram Reels, TikTok, Facebook

---

### Idea 2: "Your Competitor's Website vs. Yours (Honest Comparison)"
**Format:** Before/After carousel or side-by-side video (15 sec)
**Hook:** "Your competitor spent money on web design. Did you?"
**Content:** Real competitor sites (anonymized) + C-Design client wins
**Why Viral:** Competitive angle + social proof + local appeal
**Platforms:** Facebook, Instagram Stories + Ads, LinkedIn

---

### Idea 3: "Web Design Myths DEBUNKED" (Series)
**Format:** Quick Reel series (15 sec each, 3-4 parts)
**Hook:** Myth vs. Reality
- Myth: "Expensive = Better" | Reality: Good design is process-driven
- Myth: "Design is just pretty colors" | Reality: Design = User experience
- Myth: "I don't need a website, I have Facebook" | Reality: [Stat about Google visibility]

**Why Viral:** Educational + opinion-driven + series building (sequels = repeat views)
**Platforms:** TikTok (huge for debunk content), Instagram Reels, Facebook

---

### Idea 4: "The $300 Website vs. $3000 Website"
**Format:** Detailed comparison Reel (30-60 sec) + Carousel post
**Hook:** "Not all websites are equal. Here's what you actually get."
**Content:** 
- $300 (your Pachet Startup): What's included, deliverables, timeline, support
- $3000 (custom premium): What changes, complexity, features
- Why both work for different businesses

**Why Viral:** Price transparency + removes buyer confusion + builds trust
**Platforms:** All platforms, especially Facebook Ads

---

### Idea 5: "Site-ul Vechi Costă Bani" (The Cost of Old Websites)
**Format:** Story-driven Reel (30 sec) + LinkedIn article
**Hook:** "Every day your website stays outdated, here's how many customers you're losing..."
**Content:** 
- Statistic-driven: Bounce rate %, mobile traffic, conversion rates
- Real impact: "Your competitor is stealing your clients"
- Solution: C-Design redesign path

**Why Viral:** Fear-based motivation + provides solution + urgency
**Platforms:** Facebook (Ads + organic), LinkedIn (thought leadership angle)

## 7. PORTOFOLIU AS SOCIAL PROOF — INTEGRATION STRATEGY

### A. Portfolio Showcase on Social Media (Not Just Website)

**Instagram Portfolio Grid:**
- Dedicate 50% of posts to portfolio highlights
- Each post: Client name (if permitted) + problem + solution + results
- Format: Carousel (3-5 slides) showing design process → final result

**Facebook Albums:**
- Create "Client Portfolio" album (full-width)
- Regular posts featuring client sites
- Encourage shares: "Recognize yourself? Tag your business!"

**LinkedIn Portfolio Carousel:**
- Monthly "Portfolio Spotlight" series
- Deep-dive case study + measurable outcomes
- Comment section: Prospect conversations start here

### B. Social Proof Integration Points

**In Every Post:**
- Include client count, years in business, awards/recognition
- Examples:
  - "47 websites live | 12 five-star reviews | 2017-2026"
  - "Design award winner 2024 | Featured in [publication]"

**Video Testimonials as Primary Content:**
- Client 1-minute video: "How C-Design changed my business"
- 3-4 testimonials, rotated in Reels
- Higher engagement than text testimonials

**Trust Badges:**
- Sidebar graphic: "5.0 stars | 12 reviews | 21-day guarantee"
- Link to Google Reviews / testimonials page

**Case Study Numbers (Visible in Every Post Caption):**
- "This website generated 45 client inquiries in month 1"
- "47% increase in mobile traffic after redesign"
- "From 0 to 1,200 monthly visitors in 6 months"

### C. Portfolio-Specific Content Pillars

**Monthly Feature:** "Client Success Metrics"
- Pie chart: "How our clients use their websites" (e-commerce, lead gen, brand)
- Stat: "Average engagement rate increase: X%"
- CTA: "Want these results for your business?"

**Quarterly Trend Report:**
- "2026 Website Design Trends — Real Examples from Our Portfolio"
- Link to 3-5 client projects exemplifying trends
- Position C-Design as industry leader
