Frequently asked questions

Why does it start from 300€? What determines the final price?
The 300€ price covers the basic package with a simple 3–5 page website, logo, domain, hosting and social media. The price may increase depending on the number of pages, special functionality (online shop, bookings, etc.) or logo complexity. You will receive the exact price after your free consultation.
How long does delivery of the complete package take?
We typically deliver the complete package within 7–14 working days from order confirmation, depending on how quickly you send us your materials (text, photos). The logo is delivered within the first 3–5 days.
What happens after the first year of hosting?
After the first year you can renew hosting and the domain at the provider's standard price (usually 50–80€/year). We will notify you in advance and you are under no obligation to continue with us.
Can I choose which social media networks I want?
Yes. We usually set up Facebook + Instagram, but we can work with any combination: TikTok, LinkedIn, Google Business, etc. We decide together what suits your business best.
Can I add extra features later?
Absolutely. The website is built to grow alongside your business. We can add an online shop, blog, booking system or anything else you need at a later stage.
