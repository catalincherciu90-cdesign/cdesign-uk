Frequently asked questions

Which e-commerce platform is right for my online shop?

It depends on your goals. WooCommerce is ideal if you want full control and an easy-to-manage WordPress store, Shopify suits businesses that want a reliable, scalable hosted platform, and PrestaShop works well for larger product catalogues. We assess your products, budget and how you like to work, then recommend the best fit — there is no one-size-fits-all answer.

What payment methods can my customers use?

We set up secure Stripe checkout as standard, which accepts all major debit and credit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay. We can also add PayPal, one-off payments and recurring subscription billing, plus UK-friendly options like Klarna where they suit your business.

How many products can I sell on my store?

There is no practical limit. We build stores that handle anything from a handful of products to thousands, with categories, variations, stock control and search that stay fast as your catalogue grows. We structure everything so it is easy to add and manage products yourself.

Are there ongoing costs after the shop is built?

Yes, a few. You will have hosting and a domain, and payment providers like Stripe charge a small transaction fee per sale. Platforms such as Shopify have their own monthly plan. Beyond that, we offer optional care plans for updates, backups and support — but the store is yours, with honest fixed pricing and no hidden lock-in.

How long does it take to build an e-commerce website?

A typical small-to-medium online shop takes around 14 days once we have your products and branding, though larger catalogues or custom features take a little longer. We agree a clear timeline before we start so you always know what to expect.
