Frequently asked questions

Do I need a website care plan?

If your website matters to your business, a care plan is strongly recommended. Software, plugins and themes need regular updates to stay secure and compatible. A care plan keeps everything patched, backed up and monitored so small issues are caught before they become downtime or a security breach.

What's included in a monthly plan?

Our monthly plans typically cover managed hosting, a Cloudflare CDN and SSL, regular software and plugin updates, off-site backups, security monitoring, uptime checks and a set allowance of content changes and support each month. We tailor the plan to your site, so you only pay for what you need.

Can you host my existing website?

Yes. We can migrate most existing websites, including WordPress, WooCommerce and other common platforms, onto our managed hosting. We handle the full transfer, test everything on a staging copy and switch over with minimal or no downtime, so your visitors never notice the move.

How often are backups taken?

We take automated daily backups of your files and database as standard, stored securely off-site and retained for a rolling period. For busy or e-commerce sites we can increase the frequency, and we always take a fresh backup before any update or change so we can restore quickly if needed.

What is your response time for support?

Support requests are handled during our working hours, Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00. We aim to respond to routine requests within one business day, while urgent issues such as a site being down are prioritised and dealt with as quickly as possible.
