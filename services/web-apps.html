Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between a web app and a website?

A website mainly presents information — pages about your business, services and contact details. A web app lets people do things: log in, enter and manage data, make bookings, process orders or run reports. If your users need to interact with a system rather than just read a page, you need a web app.

Can it integrate with the tools I already use?

Yes. Most of our web apps connect to existing systems through APIs — accounting software, payment providers such as Stripe, email platforms, calendars and CRMs. This means data moves automatically between tools instead of being re-entered by hand.

How long does a custom web app take to build?

It depends on complexity. A focused tool or booking system can take a few weeks, while a larger CRM or portal with multiple integrations takes longer. After our discovery and scoping stage we give you a realistic timeline before work begins.

Do you provide maintenance after launch?

Yes. Software needs ongoing care, so we offer support and care plans covering updates, security, backups and bug fixes, plus the option to add new features as your business grows.

Do I own the code once it's finished?

Yes. Once the project is paid for, the custom code we build for you is yours. We hand over what you need and can document the system so you're never locked in.
