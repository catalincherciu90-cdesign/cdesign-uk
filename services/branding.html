Frequently asked questions

What do I get with a branding project?

You get a complete brand package: your logo in multiple formats, a colour palette, typography, a brand guidelines document and the core marketing assets you need to launch. The exact deliverables are agreed with you at the start.

How many logo concepts do you present?

We typically present two to three distinct logo concepts, each with a clear rationale. You then choose one direction, which we refine through a couple of rounds until it is exactly right.

Do I own the logo and files?

Yes. Once the project is paid in full, you own the final brand assets outright and receive the source and export files, so you are free to use them however you like.

How long does branding take?

A logo and core identity usually takes around two to four weeks, depending on scope and how quickly feedback comes back. A larger rebrand with extensive assets can take longer, and we will give you a clear timeline up front.

Can you match my branding to my website?

Absolutely. We build websites too, so we can align your new identity with your site, or design the brand with your existing website in mind, so everything looks consistent.
