## QUICK REFERENCE: BEST PRACTICES

### CTA (Call-to-Action)
Every post needs one:
- "Comment your biggest website challenge → I'll reply personally"
- "DM for free website audit"
- "Link in bio for free consultation"
- "What's missing from your website? A, B, or C?"

### Hashtag Strategy
**Instagram:** 25-30 per post (mix of high/mid/niche)
**Facebook:** 1-3 max
**LinkedIn:** 1-3 max
**TikTok:** 5-8

### Best Times to Post
- **Facebook:** 10am, 6pm (Tue-Thu)
- **Instagram:** 9am, 6pm (Tue-Wed-Thu)
- **LinkedIn:** 8am, 12pm (Mon-Wed)
- **TikTok:** 6-9pm (anytime)

### Engagement Ratio Targets
- Educational content: 3-5% engagement
- Promotional content: 2-3% engagement
- Video/Reels: 4-8% engagement

## CONTENT BATCHING WORKFLOW

**Efficient 5-day system:**
1. Day 1 (3 hours): Gather assets (photos, videos, testimonials)
2. Day 2 (2 hours): Write all copy (Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram)
3. Day 3 (2 hours): Design (carousels, infographics)
4. Day 4 (3 hours): Edit videos (testimonials, Reels, TikTok)
5. Day 5 (2 hours): Schedule everything + setup ads

**Total: 12 hours every 4 weeks = 3 hours/week**

## RED FLAGS (What NOT to Do)

❌ Post without CTA
❌ Post once then disappear
❌ Ignore comments/DMs
❌ Copy competitor content directly
❌ Post same content on all platforms (same format)
❌ Use more than 30 hashtags
❌ Post on multiple platforms then never engage
❌ Go 2+ weeks without posting
❌ Spend all budget on ads, no organic
❌ Measure only likes (measure engagement: comments, shares, clicks)

| Platform | Strength | Weakness | Best For | Posting Schedule |
