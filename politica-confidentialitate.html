We are a web design agency providing website creation, redesign, graphic design and maintenance services for small businesses. Through this website you can request a free consultation and book an appointment.

02What data we collect

We collect only the data you voluntarily enter into the booking/contact form on the website. Specifically, this includes:

  • Name – so we know how to contact you and address you correctly;
  • Phone number – to confirm the booking and discuss the details of your enquiry;
  • Email address – for correspondence and to send you a quote or confirmation;
  • Preferred date and time – to arrange the consultation;
  • Message / enquiry details – any information you choose to include about your project (optional).

We do not request or collect sensitive data (such as health information, political or religious opinions, etc.) and we do not ask for payment details through this form.

03Why we collect data

We use your data solely to:

  • respond to your consultation and information requests;
  • confirm and manage bookings made through the website;
  • contact you to discuss your project details and provide a quote.

We do not use your data for any other purpose and we never sell it to third parties.

04Legal basis

The processing of your personal data is based on your consent, given at the moment you complete and submit the booking form, in accordance with Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR.

You may withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out prior to withdrawal. To do so, please write to us at office@c-design.ro.

05How long we retain data

We retain your personal data for a period of 12 months from the date of our last interaction with you (for example, the last message, call or booking). After this period, data is securely deleted or anonymised.

Should you become a client and a contract be concluded, we may retain certain data for a longer period to the extent required by legal obligations (for example, accounting and tax obligations). We will inform you separately in such cases.

07Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

  • Right of access – to find out what data we hold about you and to receive a copy of it;
  • Right to rectification – to have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected;
  • Right to erasure (the “right to be forgotten”) – to request that we delete your data;
  • Right to data portability – to receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format;
  • Right to restriction of processing – to request that we limit the way in which we use your data;
  • Right to object – to object to the processing of your data in certain circumstances.

To exercise any of these rights, please write to us at office@c-design.ro. We will respond to your request within the timeframes required by law (generally within 30 days).

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP) – www.dataprotection.ro.

08Cookies

Our website uses cookies (small files stored in your browser) in order to function correctly and to measure performance. We use the following categories:

  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics) – these help us understand, on an anonymous basis, how the website is used (pages visited, duration, traffic source);
  • Marketing cookies (Meta Pixel) – used for remarketing and to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns.

These cookies are not essential to the basic functioning of the website. You can refuse or disable cookies at any time, either through your browser settings or via the consent banner displayed on your first visit. Refusing analytics and marketing cookies does not affect your access to the website's content.
