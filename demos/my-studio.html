Meet your teacher

Elena Hartley

Founder & Lead Instructor

Elena opened My Studio with a simple belief: that a mindful practice should feel nourishing, never intimidating. Trained across India and the UK, she blends classical alignment with a soft, intuitive touch.

Her classes are known for their calming pace, thoughtful sequencing and the space they hold for you to simply arrive and breathe.

  • Yin & Restorative
