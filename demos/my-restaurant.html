Our Story

Rooted in the seasons, plated with intention.

Since opening our doors, My Restaurant has been a quiet celebration of British produce. We work with a handful of trusted farms and day-boat fishermen, letting the ingredients decide the menu long before we do.

Our kitchen changes with the calendar — asparagus in spring, game in autumn — so no two visits are ever quite the same. Expect warm service, an unhurried pace, and a wine list chosen to match.

— Head Chef & the Team
