Our Story

Slow fashion, made to last.

My Boutique began as a small studio with a simple belief: that fewer, better things bring more joy than a crowded rail. Every piece is chosen for its cut, its cloth and the way it feels the tenth time you wear it.

We work with a handful of European ateliers, producing in small batches to reduce waste and keep quality uncompromising.
